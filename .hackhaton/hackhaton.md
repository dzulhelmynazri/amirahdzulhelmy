## What you get

Every approved participant receives a free 2-month Qoder seat, plus access to Atlas's flight APIs and curated travel datasets — unlocking content and insights from 140+ airlines across tens of thousands of origin-destination (O&D) pairs.

## Who can join

Open to all backgrounds. Participate solo or in a team of up to 3. No travel-industry experience required.

## Hackathon themes

One question drives this hackathon: what could a new beginning look like with Agentic AI — and where is growth coming from? Build against any of these tracks:

- **Hotels & Hospitality** — Autonomous guest concierges, smart property management, dynamic pricing and revenue management.
- **Flights & Aviation** — Proactive disruption handling and rebooking, intelligent routing, seamless multi-modal journeys.
- **Attractions & Destinations** — Context-aware discovery, dynamic crowd management, immersive AR guides.
- **Activities & Experiences** — Autonomous itinerary orchestration, hyper-personalised local matching, real-time language and cultural bridges.
- **Payments & Fintech** — Agentic commerce and instant checkout, B2B payment reconciliation, dynamic budget management.
- **Data & Analytics** — Predictive travel intelligence, unified customer profiles, operational insight agents.
- **AI Agent Startups & Ecosystem** — Multi-agent orchestration platforms, trust and verification systems, no-code agent builders for travel SMBs.
- **Other Travel Innovations** — Generative media and content, advanced conversational interfaces, sustainable travel agents, or anything else that moves the industry forward.
